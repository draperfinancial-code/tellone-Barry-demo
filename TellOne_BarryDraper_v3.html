My Story

Why I believe

I wanted to share something personal with you—not a sermon, and not an argument. Just my story.

Barry's Story

I was attending a Baptist church nine months before I was born! My great-grandfather was a Baptist pastor, and my mother brought that heritage into my life. From my earliest memories, I always loved Jesus.

But at eleven, some church playmates confronted me about my own sin: candy I had pocketed without paying, disobeying my parents, lying. For the first time, I understood that sin separated me from the Jesus I loved.

That Sunday afternoon I begged my mother to let me talk to Pastor Foust, who lived just a few doors away. She told me not to disturb him and to wait until church that evening.

I weighed my mother's command against eternity—and decided I would commit one more sin. Off to the pastor's house I went.

I confessed everything to Pastor Foust—including that I had disobeyed my mother just to come see him. He opened his Bible and lovingly explained, “Barry, this is why Jesus came.” Jesus died to take my sin and rose again to conquer it. Although millions could receive His gift, He did it as if it were JUST FOR ME.

Right there in his living room, in the middle of my latest sin, I bowed my head—and more importantly, my heart—and asked Jesus to forgive me and come live in my heart.

I had sung “Into My Heart” since preschool. Now I finally understood what it meant personally.

Through the years Jesus has shown His love to me in more ways than I could tell you here. I hope you'll take the next step and hear His Story. Perhaps it will become your story, too.

God created you, loves you, and desires a relationship with you.

But sin separates us from God. We cannot remove that separation by being good enough, religious enough, or trying harder.

Jesus came to earth—God as Man. He gave His own human life on the Cross, dying for your sin.

After three days, He arose from death—offering you forgiveness of sin and eternal life.

That gift is not earned. It is received by faith.
