My Story

How Jesus became personal to me

I was attending a Baptist church nine months before I was born! My great-grandfather was a Baptist pastor, and my mother brought that heritage into my life. From my earliest memories, I always loved Jesus.

But when I was eleven, some playmates—kids I went to church with— confronted me about something I had never really faced: my own sin.

There was candy I had pocketed without paying for it. I had ridden my bicycle to the convenience store without my parents' permission. I had lied. And my friends reminded me of what the revival evangelist had preached about sin and eternity.

That was a heavy conversation for an eleven-year-old. But deep down, for the first time, I knew they were right. I realized I had a problem separating me from the Jesus I had always loved. That problem was sin.

It was Sunday afternoon. Our pastor, Pastor Foust, lived in the church pastorium just a few doors away. Upset, I begged my mother to let me go talk to him.

“Barry, it's Sunday. We'll be back at church in a few hours. Don't you dare go down there and disturb him right now!”

I weighed my mother's command against the prospect of eternity in hell— and decided I would commit one more sin. Off to the pastor's house I went.

Pastor Foust welcomed me in. I told him everything: “Pastor, I've stolen things. I've been a liar. Why, I disobeyed my mother just to come down here and talk with you.”

He lovingly picked up a Bible and explained:

“Barry, this is why Jesus came.”

Jesus chose to die on the Cross to take your sin. He rose again to conquer sin on your behalf. And although millions could receive this gift, He did it as if it were JUST FOR YOU.

I understood. Right there in my pastor's living room—in the middle of my latest sin—I bowed my head, and more importantly my heart, and asked Jesus to forgive my sin and come live in my heart.

Even as a preschooler I had sung, “Into my heart… come into my heart, Lord Jesus.” But now I understood what those words meant personally. My sin separated me from the Jesus I loved—and Jesus had done everything necessary to bring me to Him.

Through the years, Jesus has demonstrated His love to me in more ways than I could possibly tell you here.

I hope you'll take the next step and hear His Story. Perhaps it will become your story, too.

His Story

The story that changed mine

The center of the Christian faith is not what we do for God, but what God has done for us in Jesus Christ.

God created you, loves you, and desires a relationship with you.

But sin separates us from God. We cannot remove that separation by being good enough, religious enough, or trying harder.

Jesus came to earth—God as Man. He gave His own human life on the Cross, dying for your sin.

After three days, He arose from death—offering you forgiveness of sin and eternal life.

That gift is not earned. It is received by faith.
