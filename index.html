Radiant Beams

Verse 1

Mary stood by the straw,
Hands still from the birth.
A little barn in Bethlehem,
The hush of holy earth.
She looked up at the star above,
Then back down where He lay,
And saw the light come pouring in
Like dawn on Christmas Day.

Chorus

Radiant beams, radiant beams
Falling soft and true
Radiant beams, radiant beams
Shining on us through
On the manger, on the wood,
On the child so small and good
Radiant beams, radiant beams
The promise coming through

Verse 2

The beams ran through the rafters,
Lit the rough-hewn stall,
And Mary saw a shadow line
Stretch long across it all.
It crossed the manger cradle,
Dark and thin and plain,
Like the shape of sorrow waiting
In the mercy of His name.

Bridge

She held Him close and trembling,
Knew the joy and ache
That love would one day carry Him
To what He came to take.
Still the star kept shining,
Still the stable glowed,
And grace lay bright in baby eyes
Where the whole wide world would know.

Ending

Radiant beams, radiant beams
Over me and you
From the manger to the cross beam
Love was shining through

THE LIGHT OF CHRISTMAS

God came to dwell
among us.

“The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it.” John 1:5

At Christmas, Catholics celebrate more than the birth of a remarkable child. We celebrate the mystery that God came near. Jesus Christ—truly God and truly man—entered our world in humility, born of the Virgin Mary and laid in a manger.

He came to reveal the Father’s love, forgive sin and open the way to eternal life. The wood of the manger points toward the wood of the Cross—but the Cross was not the end. Jesus rose, and His light still shines.

YOUR FIRST VISIT

What should I expect
at Mass?

You are genuinely welcome. No one expects a first-time guest to know every prayer, response or gesture.

01

Come as you are

Respectful, ordinary clothing is perfectly appropriate. Arrive a little early and an usher can help you find a seat.

02

Simply follow along

The congregation will stand, sit and kneel at different times. Participate as you feel comfortable.

03

Word and Eucharist

Mass includes Scripture, a homily, prayer and the Liturgy of the Eucharist. It ordinarily lasts about an hour.

04

About Holy Communion

Guests who are not receiving Holy Communion are welcome to remain in the pew and pray. Please follow the parish’s guidance.
